Who We Are

Episteme Learning Academy is a learning community built on the belief that true education goes beyond textbooks. We exist to nurture the mind, refine character, and guide learners toward purposeful living.

Blending academic excellence with moral and spiritual development, we provide a balanced educational experience for children, teenagers, and adults, within a supportive, learner-centered environment.

Meet the Founder

Episteme Learning Academy was founded by Salman Abdullateef O., a graduate of the University of Ilorin and Markazi Nabiyyi Arabic and Islamic Learning Center with a background in Plant Biology and a deep passion for educational development.

Driven by the belief that true education shapes both intellect and character, he established Episteme to provide balanced learning rooted in knowledge, discipline, and moral clarity.

His vision is to raise learners who are confident, spiritually grounded, intellectually sharp, and socially responsible.

Our Vision

To raise confident, morally grounded, and intellectually curious learners who are equipped to thrive academically, think critically, and contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

  • Deliver quality, flexible, and engaging education
  • Integrate academic learning with values and character development
  • Support learners at their own pace
  • Make education accessible, practical, and meaningful

Our Educational Philosophy

At Episteme, learning is holistic. Education should develop the mind, shape character, and prepare learners for real-life challenges—not just exams.

We teach with patience, clarity, and intention—meeting learners where they are and guiding them toward growth.
